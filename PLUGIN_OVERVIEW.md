Hmm Themes gives bb looks that feel like a different app, without moving
anything around. Same sidebar, same threads, same composer, new personality.

Pick a theme in Settings → Appearance. Each one handles light and dark mode.

- Brutalist: paper, ink and one loud yellow. Hard borders, hard shadows, no
  rounded corners, and buttons that press down when you click them.
- Apple: the calm Mac look. System font, soft shadows, capsule buttons and
  blue message bubbles.
- Paper: warm paper and serif reading text, like a well-set book.
- Retro OS: grey bevelled buttons and navy title bars from the 90s desktop.
- Terminal: green text on black, monospace everywhere, with faint scanlines.
- Liquid Glass: frosted panels floating over a soft colour gradient.
